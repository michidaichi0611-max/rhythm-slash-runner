# ストア掲載文（Google Play / App Store）

文字数の上限: Google Play はタイトル30・簡単な説明80・詳しい説明4000。App Store はアプリ名30・サブタイトル30・キーワード100（カンマ区切り）・説明4000。

素材:
- スクリーンショット 1080×1920（各6枚）: `store/screenshots/ja/`, `store/screenshots/en/`（作り方 `tools/store/make_screenshots.py`）
- 紹介動画 1080×1920・約32秒・音あり: `store/video/preview_ja.mp4`, `store/video/preview_en.mp4`（作り方 `tools/store/make_video.py`）。Google Play は YouTube に上げたURLを登録、App Store はアプリプレビューとして直接アップロード（App Store は端末ごとの解像度の指定があるので、必要なら書き出し直す）
- アイコン 1024×1024: `unity_project/Assets/RhythmRunner/Icons/icon_app.png`／Google Play 用 512×512: `store/icon_512.png`
- フィーチャーグラフィック 1024×500（Google Play で必須）: `store/feature_graphic.png`（作り方 `tools/store/make_feature_graphic.py`）

## English

**Title (30)**: Rhythm Slash Runner

**Short description (80)**: Slice to the beat! A hamster runner rhythm game — tap in time and beat the boss.

**Subtitle (App Store, 30)**: Slice to the beat, run & tap

**Keywords (App Store, 100)**: rhythm,music,beat,slash,slice,hamster,runner,tap,boss,casual,arcade,fruit,cute

**Full description**

Strawberries, watermelons and crates fly at a running hamster —
tap left, center or right in time with the music and slice them all!

■ Simple to play, great to feel
• Just tap three lanes. Hit the beat for a PERFECT!
• Hold to chop long objects, mash to smash the tough ones
• Chain combos into FEVER and race down a rainbow road

■ Bosses with personality
• The Onion King, the Toaster Fiend, the Wind-up King, the Snowman General…
• Slash their shots right back at them, then mash to finish them off!

■ A world that changes as you run
• Kitchen, Toy Room, Sky Orchard, Neon Market, Candy Land, Freezer
• The tempo rises with every stage you clear

■ Collect and dress up
• 10 characters: Shiba, Penguin, Jelly Shark, Invisible Kid…
• 9 weapons, from a cardboard sword to a smoke bat (looks and sounds only — timing is the same for everyone)

■ Something new every day
• Daily goals, login bonuses and a weekly ranking
• Share your result card and challenge your friends

New to rhythm games? You start with a short practice, and every new thing comes with practice time.

* Contains flashing lights; effect strength can be changed in Settings.
* Contains in-app purchases (energy drinks).
